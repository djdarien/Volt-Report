Charging & Battery Info

Battery Options & Range

  • Standard Range Plus (Model 3/Y): ~260-280 mi EPA
  • Long Range (All Models): ~300-358 mi EPA depending on model
  • Plaid (S/X): ~400+ mi EPA with ultimate performance
  • Cybertruck (Tri-Motor): ~320 mi EPA with 14,000 lbs towing
  • Home Charging (Level 2): Add ~30-35 mi per hour charged
  • Supercharging: Add 200+ mi in 15-20 minutes at 10-80% charge

Real-World Tips for Tesla Owners

Battery Health & Longevity

  • Tesla batteries are warrantied for 8 years or 120k-200k miles depending on model
  • Charge to 80% daily for optimal battery health; full charge only before long trips
  • Preconditioning battery 15-20 min before DC fast charging improves efficiency
  • Cold weather reduces range 20-40%; warm up cabin with seat heaters instead of AC
  • Avoid frequent supercharging; use home Level 2 charging when possible
