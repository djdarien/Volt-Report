Safety & Responsibility

⚠️ Critical Safety Information

  • Remain Attentive: Autopilot and FSD are driver-assistance features, not autonomous driving. You must monitor and be ready to take control at any time.
  • Keep Hands on Wheel: The system detects hand pressure. Remove hands for extended periods and the car will warn you and gradually disengage.
  • Understand Limitations: Neither system works in all conditions (heavy rain, snow, poor visibility, missing lane markings, etc.).
  • Weather Matters: FSD Beta's vision-based system is affected by weather. Be extra cautious in rain or snow.
  • Construction Zones: Both systems can be confused by temporary lane markings. Manually navigate construction areas when possible.
  • Your Responsibility: You are liable for the vehicle's actions. Tesla's terms require you to be responsible for safe operation.

✓ Best Practices

  • Test new features on familiar roads first, not on critical highway trips.
  • Check Tesla's official documentation and YouTube channel for the latest features and updates.
  • Join Tesla owner communities and Reddit forums to learn real-world experiences.
  • Keep your car's software up-to-date for latest safety patches and improvements.
  • On FSD beta, use it in low-traffic areas first to understand its behavior.
  • Always assume the car may not recognize an obstacle or situation correctly.

Latest Updates & Beta Releases

FSD V12 & Beyond

Latest versions focus on vision-only architecture

Tesla transitioned to a camera-based system removing radar. New versions emphasize improved object detection and prediction under various conditions.

Latest features: Improved street navigation, better handling of edge cases, smoother driving behavior.

Continuous Improvement

Rolling updates

Tesla releases FSD beta updates roughly every 1-2 weeks. Beta testers report improvements through Tesla's feedback system.

Current focus: Reducing false positives, improving lane keeping, better pedestrian prediction.

Hardware Support

Applies to all Tesla vehicles

Even older Tesla vehicles (2016+) with proper hardware can access Autopilot features. Some advanced FSD features require HW3.0+ (newer computers).

Tip: Check your car's hardware version in Settings to see which features you're eligible for.

Comparing to Competitors

How Tesla's Autopilot and FSD compare to other manufacturers:

  • Supercruise (GM/Cadillac): More conservative, works only on highway mapping (limited roads). Doesn't include city street autonomy.
  • Super Intelligent Pilot (Xpeng): Advanced on highways, expanding city street capability. Chinese focus, limited US availability.
  • BlueCruise (Ford): Similar to Supercruise. Hands-off highway driving on pre-mapped roads only.
  • Waymo Driver (Alphabet): Fully autonomous ride-hailing in select cities. Not consumer-facing for personal vehicles yet.
  • Tesla FSD: Most ambitious consumer offering with city streets, parking, and continuous over-the-air updates.
