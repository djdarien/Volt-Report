FSD v14.1 Lite (HW3 / AI3)

v14 Lite landed in June 2026. v14.1 Lite followed in August (2026.21.6). v14.2 Lite started appearing in mid-September.

  • Distilled HW4 v14 behavior onto HW3 compute
  • Start FSD from Park, Arrival Options, always-on Speed Profiles (including SLOTH)
  • Parking, unparking, and reversing
  • Still supervised Level 2 -- stay attentive

Safety

FSD is driver assistance, not autonomy. Keep hands ready, watch the road, and take over in rain, construction, or anything unclear.
