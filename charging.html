Types of EV Charging

Level 1 (120V)

3-5 miles/hour. Standard household outlet. Overnight or emergency top-ups.

Level 2 (240V)

25-30 miles/hour. Home or public charger. Full charge in 4-10 hours.

DC Fast Charging

150-350 miles/hour. 10-80% in 20-45 minutes. Superchargers and public networks.

Home Charging Setup

Installation basics

  • Cost: $500-$2,500 for equipment plus installation
  • Time: 4-8 hours for a standard home setup
  • Electrical: 30-60 amp circuit
  • Speed: about 25-30 miles per hour

Many utilities offer rebates. Federal credit may cover part of a qualifying charger.

Public networks

Tesla Supercharger

150-250 kW. Best North America coverage. About $0.26/kWh or subscription.

Electrify America

150-350 kW. About $0.35/kWh or membership.

EVgo

50-350 kW in cities and highways. About $0.30/kWh.

ChargePoint

Largest public network. Mostly Level 2, DC fast growing. Pricing varies.

Charging costs

  • Home Level 2: about $0.14-0.18/kWh
  • Tesla Supercharger: about $0.26/kWh
  • Electrify America DC: about $0.35/kWh
  • Example Model 3 at 1,000 miles/month home charging: about $38/month vs $120-180 gas

Use ABRP or the car nav for road trips. Charge to about 80% on DC fast. Check PlugShare before a detour.
