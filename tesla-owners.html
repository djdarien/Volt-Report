Warranty & Protection

🛡️ Tesla Warranty Coverage

  • Battery & Drive Unit: 8 years or 120,000-200,000 miles (varies by model) - 100% coverage
  • Bumper-to-Bumper: 4 years or 50,000 miles - covers most components except wear items
  • Paint: 4 years - protection against defects
  • Roadside Assistance: Complimentary 24/7 support for first 4 years
  • Regional Variations: Warranty terms may differ by country. Check your local Tesla site.

What's covered under bumper-to-bumper warranty?

Almost everything except normal wear items (tires, wiper blades, brake pads). This includes the touchscreen, seals, gaskets, hardware, and most mechanical parts.

Can I extend my warranty?

Yes, Tesla offers extended service plans. Talk to your service center or check your Tesla app for available extension options and pricing.

How much battery degradation should I expect?

Most Teslas retain 90-95% of original battery capacity after 8 years/120k miles. Warranty covers degradation below specified levels (typically 70% minimum).

Software Updates Regular (automatic) Keep car updated for latest features and security patches

Note: Tesla vehicles require significantly less maintenance than gas cars. No oil changes, transmission fluid, or spark plugs needed. Regenerative braking means brake service is rare.

Battery Health & Longevity Tips

📊 How to Check Battery Health

In your Tesla: Controls → Service → Battery to see current health percentage and estimated range. Most owners see 2-3% degradation per 100,000 miles.

⚡ Optimal Charging Habits

Daily: Charge to 80% for maximum battery longevity. Long trips: Charge to 100% only before road trips. Storage: Keep battery between 20-80% if storing for extended periods.

❄️ Cold Weather Impact

Cold reduces range 20-40% temporarily. The battery reheats itself when driving. Use preconditioning before trips and seat heaters instead of cabin heat to preserve range.

🔋 Supercharging Strategy

Occasional supercharging is fine, but frequent DC fast charging can accelerate battery degradation. Use home charging (Level 2) for daily use and Superchargers mainly for road trips.

🌡️ Hot Weather Concerns

Park in shade or use pre-cooling in the app to keep battery cool. Extreme heat for prolonged periods can impact longevity, though modern Teslas manage this automatically.
